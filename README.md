# 🪔 Diwali Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** built using a Diwali sales dataset. 
The dashboard helps analyze customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, and business trends during the Diwali season.

It enables stakeholders to quickly identify high-performing customer segments, regions, and sales patterns through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top customers and high-revenue states
- Understand customer demographics
- Discover purchasing patterns
- Track monthly sales trends
- Support business decisions with data-driven insights

---

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard provides key business metrics such as:

- Total Revenue
- Customer Rating
- Rating Score
- Interactive filters for deeper analysis

---

# 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

## 1. Revenue by Customer (Clustered Column Chart)

**Purpose**
- Displays revenue generated by each customer.

**Insights**
- Identifies top-spending customers.
- Helps businesses recognize loyal customers.
- Useful for personalized marketing campaigns.

---

## 2. Orders by Occupation (Donut Chart)

**Purpose**
- Shows the distribution of orders across different occupations.

**Insights**
- Reveals which profession contributes the highest number of purchases.
- Helps identify the most valuable customer segment.

---

## 3. Revenue by Age Segment (Pie Chart)

**Purpose**
- Shows revenue contribution from different age groups.

**Insights**
- Identifies the age segment generating the highest sales.
- Helps target marketing campaigns toward the most profitable customers.

---

## 4. Monthly Revenue Trend (Column Chart)

**Purpose**
- Displays revenue generated each month.

**Insights**
- Identifies peak sales months.
- Highlights seasonal purchasing trends.
- Helps forecast future sales.

---

## 5. Revenue by State (Clustered Bar Chart)

**Purpose**
- Compares revenue generated across different states.

**Insights**
- Identifies top-performing states.
- Helps businesses focus regional marketing efforts.
- Useful for inventory planning.

---

## 6. Orders by Gender (Donut Chart)

**Purpose**
- Displays order distribution between male and female customers.

**Insights**
- Identifies which gender places more orders.
- Helps understand customer demographics.

---

## 7. Delivery Segment (Funnel Chart)

**Purpose**
- Shows the distribution of orders across delivery categories.

**Insights**
- Helps evaluate delivery performance.
- Identifies the most frequently used delivery segment.

---

## 🎛 Interactive Features

The dashboard includes slicers for filtering data dynamically, allowing users to analyze sales based on different customer attributes.

Examples:
- Gender
- Other customer categories
